KLN Textiles Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

KLN Textiles ("we", "us", or "our") operates the KLN Textiles mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

The KLN Textiles App is a business management application intended for authorized users. Depending on how the App is used, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Account information, such as email address used for authentication.
  • Worker information, including names and phone numbers.
  • Attendance information.
  • Salary and payment information.
  • Material and inventory-related information.
  • Other business information entered by authorized users through the App.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information collected through the App to provide and operate the KLN Textiles business management functionality, including:

  • Managing authorized user accounts.
  • Managing worker information.
  • Recording and managing attendance.
  • Calculating and managing salaries.
  • Recording and managing payments.
  • Managing materials and inventory information.
  • Maintaining and improving the App.
  • Protecting the security of the App and its data.

3. Account Authentication

The App uses email and password authentication to allow authorized users to access the application.

Authentication services are provided using Firebase Authentication. Authentication information is used to securely identify and authenticate users.

4. Data Storage

Business and account information is stored using cloud services provided by Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect stored information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by service providers that support the operation of the App, such as Firebase services, where necessary to provide authentication, database storage, and related infrastructure.

6. Firebase Services

The App uses Firebase services provided by Google for authentication and cloud data storage.

At the time of this policy, the App does not use Firebase Analytics for analytics tracking.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored through the App. Access to business information is intended to be limited to authorized users.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the Internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to provide the business management functionality of the App and to meet applicable business, legal, or operational requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or securely removed in accordance with applicable requirements.

9. Account and Data Deletion

Users can request deletion of their KLN Textiles account and associated personal data through the Delete Account option available in the Profile section of the App.

When an account deletion request is submitted through the App, the request will be processed in accordance with our data retention requirements.

Users may also request account and data deletion by contacting us at the email address below. Please include the email address associated with your KLN Textiles account so that we can verify the request.

Email: gowshitextiles@gmail.com

Certain business records may be retained where necessary for legitimate business, legal, security, or regulatory purposes. Any retained information will be handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for business use by authorized users and is not directed toward children.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for the purpose of providing the App.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App, our practices, or applicable requirements.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
